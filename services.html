Services

Engagements are scoped around outcomes, not hours. A few common shapes:

Fractional Head of Product

Ongoing, part-time (e.g. 1–3 days/week) product leadership for teams without a full-time PM.

Product audit

A focused review of your roadmap, process, and metrics with a concrete set of recommendations.

Launch sprint

Fixed-term, hands-on support to take a specific feature or product from concept to launch.

Interim PM coverage

Bridge a gap during parental leave, transition, or hiring — keep momentum without dropping context.

Advisory / retainer

Lighter-touch, ongoing sounding board for founders and product leads — async and periodic calls.

Team coaching

Level up an existing PM or product team with mentoring, frameworks, and hands-on pairing.
